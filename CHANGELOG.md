# Changelog

What changed for someone who uses Manor, newest first. A version is a tag on `main`. The root, server, web, desktop, contracts and client runtime packages carry the same number. The phone companion and the blobatar copy keep their own.

## Unreleased

- On the Mac and the web, resting the pointer on a face pinned in the left rail shows a small card with her name and role, the last message in your direct message with her ("You:" when you wrote it), how long ago it was, and whether she is working or waiting for you. Moving to the next face swaps the card at once, keyboard focus shows it too, and a click still opens the conversation. Looking does not mark it as read.
- A persona's face moves with its whole body while her turn works, on the Mac, the web and the phone. She leans, rocks, turns her head away and back, rolls over the top into a ball, shrinks away, looks aside, thinks and looks down, in a new order each round. This replaces the earlier work gestures (rocking while she searches, leaning while she edits, squashing on a command, shivering on a failed call, rolling while compacting). The three dots still show while she writes, and the loop comes back when the turn goes on after a written message. In the Agents list a working face plays the same loop. On the Mac and the web the spinner beside it is gone, while groups keep theirs. With reduced motion, or for a face with a picture, a working face stays still with thought dots above it, now in the Agents list too.
- On the Mac and the web, Settings → Appearance offers colour themes: Manor, Parchment, Indigo, Moss and Plum. Each theme card has two orbs, the left one for light mode and the right one for dark mode, so light and dark can come from different themes. Mode (System, Light, Dark) stays its own row above, with previews in the themes you picked. Manor stays the default and looks exactly as before. The other themes keep their tint on panels, the composer and search fields in light mode, instead of pure white. The choice is saved with your settings and survives a restart. Persona and project colours, fonts and code colours stay the same in every theme.
- On the Mac and the web, you can mark up a picture before you send it. Click a picture in the composer to place numbered pins, draw a frame or a circle, and type a comment per pin; drag a mark to move it and Undo to step back. Done shows the pin count on the picture, and clicking it again reopens your marks. The agent gets the marked picture, the original and your numbered comments, and your bubble shows the marked picture with the list. An agent's picture has Annotate on hover, which opens the same editor and puts the result in your composer. A marked picture counts as two of the eight attachments. The phone shows both pictures and the text, without the list.
- A project can wear its repository's own icon. When a linked folder holds a favicon or app icon (such as `public/favicon.svg` or `assets/icon.png`), project settings on the Mac, the web and the phone offer Repo icon as the first choice, showing the real picture. An icon without a background is preferred and drawn as it is; one that fills its square gets slightly rounded corners. Picked, the project shows it everywhere its icon appears. Manor keeps a reference to the file, not a copy: if the file is deleted or the folder unlinked, the project shows its glyph again.
- A project can wear two letters of its name instead of a glyph: the first letters of its first two words, or the first two letters of a single word (Shot Journal reads SJ, Manor reads MA). Letters is a choice in the icon picker on the Mac, the web and the phone, also for a project without a folder, and draws on a tile in the project's colour wherever its icon appears.
- On the Mac and the web, Sessions can show a live iOS Simulator beside the chat. Turn on Settings → General → iOS Simulator (off by default, needs Xcode), then open the Simulator door in a Session's right rail. Pick a device or start one that is off, click to tap, drag to swipe and type while the screen has focus. Home, Rotate, Power off and Float sit in the panel. Every panel shows the same device, and closing the panel stops the picture while the device keeps running. The first screen you open downloads a small helper. The project thread and direct messages have no Simulator door.
- On the phone, project settings open on the project itself: a large icon tile in the project colour with a pencil to change icon and colour, and the name under it, which you tap to rename. Folders sit in one card with Add a folder as its last row, Files has its own card, and Delete project thread stands apart at the bottom. Knowledge work memory is now set on the Mac only; saving from the phone leaves it as it was.
- On the phone, a large face's shadow fades out softly toward its bottom edge instead of ending in a hard rim. Faces in lists look as before.
- On the phone, every tab (Agents, Sessions, Knowledge, Search) shows a pill at the top right with a ring per signed-in provider that reports a limit, each filled to what is left of that plan's fullest limit: Claude first, then Codex, and +N for the others. Tapping it opens Usage as a sheet: per provider each limit with a bar, the share left, the reset time and a small tick where the bar would be if you spent evenly. Usage is no longer a row in the menu. A reset day reads in English whatever the device's language, on the Mac, the web and the phone.
- On the phone, a swipe to the left in a project thread brings in the project's Sessions as the next page, the way the Mac shows them beside the thread. A swipe to the right, or Back, returns to the thread where you left it. The Sessions button in the header opens the same page. A code block wider than the screen scrolls sideways under the finger instead.

## 0.3.0, 2026-11-22

- A persona's face has five new shapes to choose from: clover, flower, star, heart and ghost. Only a face you give one of them wears it, so no existing face changes shape. The cloud is redrawn as a flat-bottomed cloud with three puffs instead of a lumpy blob. The capsule no longer cuts off its eyes while it works. On the Mac, the web and the phone; the phone needs a new build.
- The desktop app has dictation. A microphone button beside send in the composer records your voice, shows the sound as it comes in, and ✓ puts the text at the cursor for you to edit; nothing is sent until you press send. ✕ or Esc throws the recording away. Speech is turned into text on your Mac by a model you download once, with no account or key. Settings → Voice lists the models (Whisper Turbo, the default and the most accurate for Dutch, and Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, which is near instant), the language you speak and the microphone to record from. The first press on the microphone offers the download.
- In a direct message, your bubbles are always a vivid colour in the hue of the face you talk to, whatever tone the face wears. Yellow and olive faces get a warm orange or a green bubble instead of a muddy brown one. On the Mac, the web and the phone.
- The light at the top left of a persona's face is fainter and spreads wider over the head, so it reads as light falling on the face instead of a spot on it. On the Mac, the web and the phone.
- A Session from a direct message brief that goes to a project thread now appears there as started, on the Mac, the web and the phone. Before, the thread briefly showed an Accept card for work you had already accepted, and its reply could say the Session waited for your Accept or name a model of its own. The Session runs on the model you chose in the direct message. If it cannot start, its card waits for Accept with the error under it.
- A Session proposal in a direct message whose project has a project thread offers only Single, like the thread's own cards, because Accept hands it to that thread. A proposal for a project without a thread, or for No project, still offers Single and Multiple. A proposal from before this change that was set to Multiple now starts as Single. On the Mac, the web and the phone, the project's name sits on the title line, right after the last word, and a long title wraps instead of being cut off.
- On desktop and web, Knowledge notes show the filename as a large title, with the folder path and editing tools above it. Note properties appear in rows with tag chips and a status pill. Click a value to edit its YAML.

- A picture dragged from the desktop onto a Knowledge note is saved in the vault's attachment folder and added to the note, wherever you let go on the note page, also beside or below the text of a short note. Before, a drop outside the lines of text did nothing. A dropped picture lands on the line after the one under the pointer, after a whole table or code block, and never inside the note's properties.
- A persona's face now moves with more of what her turn does, on the Mac, the web and the phone. On top of the moving eyes she rocks twice while she searches, leans in while she edits, squashes once when she runs a command, shivers once when a call fails and rolls once while the conversation is compacted. Plain tool calls keep the eyes alone. In a direct message her face stays under a reply that lands whole for a moment, smiles and nods, then goes; a stopped or failed turn still leaves at once. The phone needs a new build.
- On the phone, a table in a chat that is wider than the screen scrolls sideways again and reaches the screen's edges, with a soft fade on each side that still has columns out of sight. Long cell text wraps onto more lines, up to a column width, instead of being cut off with an ellipsis. Before, the table looked cut off at the edge and a swipe on it did nothing. A table that fits has no fade, and swiping to reply on the rest of a message works as before.
- Persona faces have soft upright oval eyes instead of slanted slits, with a faint sheen high in each dark eye, and about half of them carry their eyes a little higher. The finish loses the hard white dot at the top left of the head; a soft highlight stays. Every generated face changes, on the Mac, the web and the phone.
- On the phone, a plain text message you send at the end of a direct message travels from the composer into the chat. The field fills with the bubble's colour, narrows to the message's own size inside the composer, then rises into its place while the chat makes room, so a short message never shows as a wide bubble and its words are never on screen twice. The typing face grows in and a reply slides up and fades in. With Reduce Motion on, new messages only fade in.
- On the phone, swipes follow the finger and finish at the speed you let go. Swipe-to-reply, the menu, closing a full-screen picture and a zoomed picture give a little past their edges instead of stopping dead and spring back, and the reply arrow fades in as a message nears the point where it replies. A swipe the system interrupts no longer replies or opens the menu. With Reduce Motion on, they still follow the finger but settle at once.
- Geist is the default interface font on the Mac, the web and the phone, replacing Figtree, because it reads more neutral. Settings → Appearance lists Geist first and Figtree among the other fonts. A font you picked yourself stays; if you never picked one you now see Geist, and one you picked before as Geist is now simply the default. The phone needs a new build for the font change. Phone text is drawn at 94% of its stated size, so it keeps the height it had, because Geist's lowercase is taller than Figtree's.
- A Session result that leaves you questions keeps Confirm (or Close) off until each question on the card has an answer, on the Mac and on the phone; its tooltip says Pick an answer first. Answers that need no change, such as Keep them, turn Confirm on, and one click lands the result. An answer that asks for a change opens Follow up and keeps Confirm off, so the answers go to the Session with Request changes. A result without questions works as before.
- Reloading the window while Settings is open (⌘R) keeps you on the same Settings page. A setting the running server does not know yet, because the app was updated while the server kept running, no longer looks saved and then springs back: Settings says to restart Manor instead.
- A project room remembers what was open on its right. Open a Session, or a subagent at any depth, go to another screen and come back to the project thread, and the same Session or subagent is open again. A Session that was deleted or archived meanwhile brings back the Sessions overview.
- A scheduled task in an agent's direct message can now pass something on to another agent. The run keeps going and its message says whom it told, with a Messaged line. The other agent gets a Message from line in its own chat and tells you there what it will do. The exchange stays readable in the view-only chat between the two agents. That agent cannot message anyone back from that turn or use a connector that writes, and the same message to the same agent is not sent twice within 14 days.
- On the phone, a full-screen picture pinches to zoom up to 5x, pans while zoomed, and a double tap switches between fit and 2x. Letting go at or under fit springs back to fit, and swiping between pictures or down to close waits until it is back at fit. While a picture or file viewer is open it turns with the phone, and the app is upright again once it closes. The rest of the app stays portrait. This needs a new build of the phone app.
- Knowledge and Files open notes and files in tabs. A click in the sidebar replaces the active tab; ⌘-click, a middle click or a drag onto the strip opens a new one, and tabs reorder by drag. Knowledge keeps its tabs in the top bar, the Knowledge and Files panels beside a chat keep theirs per chat in the panel header. The plus opens an empty tab, which a pick from the sidebar or New note then fills. ⌘W closes the active tab while that surface has focus, and the tabs come back after a restart.
- Settings → General has a Power group on a Mac. Keep this Mac awake offers Never, While agents work and Always, and stops the Mac from going to sleep on its own while it applies. The display can still sleep and a closed lid still sleeps the Mac. While agents work lets go about a minute after the last agent stops.
- Settings → General has a Browser group in the desktop app. Search engine sets where words typed into the Browser's address bar are searched, with six engines to pick from. A pasted results page now opens as the address it is.
- Quitting the desktop app while agents are working asks first, and names how many chats would be stopped. It asks only when the app runs its own server.
- Settings → Appearance offers more fonts. Interface font adds Albert Sans, Lato, Lexend, Montserrat, Nunito Sans, Outfit, Poppins, Roboto, Rubik, Sora, Spline Sans and Work Sans, and Code font adds Spline Sans Mono. Both menus list the default first, then the rest alphabetically. Lato has no medium weight, so text that is normally medium shows in its regular weight.

## 0.2.1, 2026-11-19

- Settings, Agent settings, Connectors, Usage, the composer's skill menu and the scheduled task dialog drop the subtitles, captions, footnotes and hints that only restated what the screen already shows. A connector's tool list opens with a chevron instead of a change or hide suffix. The mymind connector explains itself in one line, and the cost figure on Usage no longer shows 100% of cost when one provider holds it all.
- Settings → Appearance has an Interface font and a Code font menu, on the Mac and in the browser. Interface font offers twelve faces, Figtree by default; Code font offers seven, SF Mono by default, and sets code blocks, inline code, diffs, the file viewer and the editors. Each menu names its fonts in their own face, and a sample under it, a message with its chips or a small diff, shows the font under the pointer before you pick. Code never joins characters such as `!=` into one glyph. The added fonts ship with Manor and work offline. The default code font is SF Mono again, where it had been Geist Mono. The phone keeps its fonts.
- A Session a project thread proposes gets one row in the project's Notes straight away, which reads proposed, then accepted, then the Session's own status. A newer version of the proposal takes over that row, Reject checks it off with the reason, and landed work closes it as before, so a proposed Session no longer leaves a second row behind that never closes. The thread can no longer add its own row for a Session that is still open. A task without a Session that stayed open for more than a week now asks the thread to check or close it.
- Settings has a Permissions page. It shows whether macOS lets Manor use Accessibility and Screen Recording, with Granted, Pending or Not granted, and a button that opens the matching System Settings pane. The status follows System Settings without a restart. In a browser the page says that permissions only apply in the desktop app. The Manor.app release now carries its own Cua driver, so computer use asks macOS for these permissions for Manor itself instead of for a separate CuaDriver app. On its first start a new Manor.app adds the computer connector by itself; remove it in Connections and it stays removed.
- The chat beside the Browser always runs one model. New session starts one straight away, without a Single or Multiple choice, and its composer has no Auto switch: it names the model and reasoning in use. A Browser chat made as Multiple before becomes a one-model chat, and one left on Auto keeps the model Auto already picked for it, or runs your default model when Auto never picked one. The model picker now stays inside the window, also when the chat is at its narrowest.
- The Usage dialog behind the rail's rings draws the same thin line on each limit bar with window timing as the Usage page: where the fill would be if you spent evenly through that window. It leaves out a provider you have not installed or signed in to, and the other cards close up.
- In the Sessions overview, a Working Session shows how far its newest tasks card is, a small ring and a count such as 2/7, on the Mac and on the phone. It replaces the age. That card can be the one Confirm shows for its commit and merge steps, so a Session that finished its plan reads 0/2 when Confirm starts and ticks along with Git. A Session with no tasks card shows nothing there.
- Connecting the phone takes three steps: Tailscale on the Mac and the phone, then scan the QR code in Settings → Phone with the iPhone camera. It opens the Manor app, which asks you to confirm the Mac before it connects. That page turns Tailscale HTTPS on and off, makes a one-time link that works for 10 minutes on one device, and lists paired devices with Revoke, which disconnects that device at once. To pair a phone's browser instead, choose Pair a browser instead under the QR code. The password account and the server address in the phone build are gone. Tailscale installed as the Mac app alone now works too, and the page never offers a localhost link, which no phone can open. A link on the Mac's network address still works.
- On the phone, the switches (such as All, Media and Files in a chat's files, the Plugins sections, Scheduled tasks and the chat mode on a new Session) are pills with one marker that slides to your choice, and it stays still when Reduce Motion is on. A switch that has a row to itself fills the row with equal parts. The mode switch on a proposal card keeps its own width, because it shares its row with a label.
- When one agent messages another, the agent that received it now knows in its own chat with you what it was told and what it answered. It no longer says no message arrived. It reads only the threads it is part of, and the exchange still lives only in that agent thread.
- On the phone, every button on a card (proposal, result, question, permission, secret, mail and Slack draft, and the file viewer's Open and Download) is Liquid Glass. The main action, such as Accept, Confirm and Send, is tinted glass so it stays the clear choice. Where glass is not available the buttons keep a flat fill.
- A Session whose turn ended while a background command or helper it started still runs now reads Waiting instead of Working, without a spinner. The project overview, on the Mac and on the phone, shows such Sessions in a Waiting section with what they wait on, such as the command's own description. The section only shows while a Session waits. When the work ends the Session picks up again and reads Working, or Done if nothing follows.
- A subagent's own view no longer shows the Session's Settle button, the button that opens the Session panel or the panel itself, even when the panel was open. Open parent or the breadcrumb brings all three back as you left them, and the panel shortcuts do nothing meanwhile. The phone's subagent screen never had them.
- When background work an agent started finishes, its line in the chat now says so in plain words, such as Background task finished: retry the phone launch, using the short description the agent gave the command. A command without one shows its program and first argument instead of the whole script, and three or more read as one count, such as 3 background tasks finished. The line stays at most two lines, on the Mac and on the phone.
- A Session in a project without a screenshot route (no verify skill, nor the skill that makes one) no longer needs a screenshot to report UI work. A passing test, typecheck or build is enough, and when no screenshot showed the change the result card says Not checked on screen. The first time such a Session works on UI, it asks once whether to add Cursor's open source verify skills to the project. Yes adds them with that Session's work. No means Manor never asks again in that project.
- On the phone, Request changes on a Session's result card takes pictures: paste one into the field and it shows as a small thumbnail with its own remove, and goes to the Session with your text. While you write, the card stands above the keyboard in place of the message field, so only one field is on screen. Cancel, Send or tapping away puts the card back in the thread where it was.
- The phone no longer flashes Offline when its connection to the Mac drops for a moment, such as when Manor restarts or the app comes back from the background. It shows Connecting… and reads Offline only after ten seconds without a connection. Coming back after more than an hour no longer fails the first reconnect on an expired sign-in, and a connection that went silent or hangs is replaced within seconds. The Mac app shows the same.
- A line in an agent's standing notes may now hold 300 characters, its date included, instead of 160. Lines saved before stay as they are.
- A project's Notes no longer show Manor's hidden source tags as text. A task that stayed open and unchanged for three days or more shows how long, such as open 4d, beside it. The project thread now reads that age on the task itself instead of a second stale copy of it above the list.
- On the phone, a project thread no longer jumps up and down when a proposal or result card arrives or changes. At the bottom it moves once to keep the card in view.
- A tasks card whose tasks are all done shows closed, as just its header with the check, the heading and the count, on the Mac and on the phone. It folds shut when the last task is ticked, and the chevron opens it again. A card you opened or closed yourself keeps that while it stays on screen, and a card that ended with tasks not done stays open.

## 0.2.0, 2026-11-17

- Pictures an agent hands over show only as pictures, without a file row with Open and Save under them. Other files, such as a PDF or a spreadsheet, keep their row. Pressing an agent's picture opens the same viewer as a DM's Files, over just that message's pictures, with Download to save one. On the Mac and on the phone.
- The cards inside a turn, questions, permission requests, secret requests, connect cards and mail and Slack drafts, set their text at 14px in every column, so they sit quieter beside the reply. In a DM every card in the chat now runs as wide as a bubble may grow, so they line up with the replies.
- The name on a pinned agent stays centred under the face when its unread dot appears or goes. The dot now hangs beside the name, on the Mac and on the phone.
- An agent adds an event to your iCloud calendar without an approval card when Manor runs with full access. With Auto it goes through the same review as other connector writes, and with Confirm it asks as before, showing the event. A scheduled routine still cannot add an event.
- A chat's time lines stand only directly above a message. Lines between agents, such as 2 messages with an agent, and hidden or system rows no longer get a time of their own, so several times no longer stack on top of each other. The phone shows the same time lines in a DM as the Mac.
- Show source on a quote, and other jumps to a message, scroll only the conversation. With the chat already at its bottom, the whole window used to slide up and leave a dark band under the message field. The source message now also lights up briefly, as a search hit does.
- The phone's Sessions screen drops its own search field; the Search tab finds every Session. The button at the bottom right now reads New session, in dark glass on the light theme and light glass on the dark theme.
- The phone app opens on the Agents tab instead of Sessions. Sessions is one tap away in the tab bar.
- Confirm on a Session that moved a file now also removes it from its old place. The commit used to add the file at its new path and leave the old copy behind, so a phone app built from such a commit could crash on launch.
- The decisions on a Session's result card read as a plain question, such as whether Manor should clean something up on its own, instead of a short internal label, on the Mac and on the phone. Their options say what happens, and an agent no longer asks you about details it can settle itself.
- A turn that changed more than 40 files shows every small file's diff. The files after the fortieth used to say partial and too large to show, even at one line. When a very wide turn does run out of room, the largest files say their diff was not loaded, and only a hunk that really is too large still says so.
- Persona faces no longer draw a shadow under them, or a glow in the dark theme. The light and shade on the face itself stay as they were, on the Mac and on the phone.
- One Stop halts the whole message. Every agent it addressed that has not started yet is cancelled too, the ones you mentioned by name included. Each used to need its own Stop.
- The Single and Multiple switch on a new Session explains itself in a tooltip when you point at it. The line under the message field is gone.
- The sidebar names its sections Project threads and Standalone sessions, on the Mac and on the phone. Adding a project thread opens New project thread, with one line on what it is.
- An agent that had been quiet for weeks answers again. Claude Code deletes a saved conversation after 30 days, and from then on every message to that agent failed at once, without a word in a DM. When Claude Code or Grok no longer has the conversation, Manor now starts a fresh session from its own record of the chat and answers the same message.
- The menus share one row: the same height and corners, an icon in front of each action, and a tick on the choice in use. That covers the Browser's menus and More panels, where a choice read Selected or Current, and the plus, model and branch menus under the message field, where the chosen row was filled.
- The New session and Recent sessions menus beside the Browser take the arrow keys, Home and End, as the other menus do. They and the Browser menu now put focus on their first row the first time they open too, not only from the second time on.
- While an agent works through its steps, only the turn that changed redraws. Every step used to redraw every message on screen, on the phone and on the Mac, a few times a second in a busy turn.
- Coming back to a chat after the connection dropped loads only what was added or changed meanwhile, on the phone and on the Mac. It used to download the whole conversation again, megabytes for a long one. After a restart of Manor on your Mac the first open still loads everything.
- On the Mac, opening a subagent's view again, or a chat in a second pane, asks only for what was added since. Each open used to download the whole thread.
- On the phone, the label next to the plus names who you mention while an agent is still answering, as on the Mac. It read as unknown for every draft during a running turn.
- On the phone, an answer typed on a question card stops at the 2,000 characters the Mac allows. A longer one made Send do nothing, without a word. A question you skipped on the Mac now reads Skipped on the phone, as it does there, and no longer the sentence meant for the agent.
- On the phone, what you type under Request changes stays when its card scrolls out of view or you leave the chat. The field used to come back open and empty, and a picked Your call choice was lost with it.
- On the phone, typing redraws only the composer. Every character used to redraw the whole chat screen, its header and its list with it.
- On the phone, a reply that streams in redraws only its own text. The header, the composer, the menu and the list under the chat used to redraw with every piece of text, in a DM too, where the reply shows only once it is whole. Scrolling and typing stay smooth while an agent writes.
- On the phone, the cards in a chat notice when the connection to your Mac drops or returns. A mail card that was already on screen kept its Send and Discard buttons live while offline.
- On the phone, a chat you went back from shows its unread dot when a reply lands in it, and a run that ends there waits for you instead of settling itself. Coming back to the app on the inbox no longer reloads the last chat you had open.
- The phone app loads a third of the code it did. It bundled every icon in the set, about 6,000, to draw 174.
- A DM's composer is one rounded row at the bottom, in an empty DM too: a plus, the message and send. It grows into the usual card once the message is longer than a line or carries an attachment. The model picker left it, because a DM runs on the model its agent's settings name. Auto no longer picks a model for a DM. A DM that still carries an earlier pick shows it in Agent settings, and choosing a model there replaces it.
- A rule you give an agent in chat, such as "write follow-ups in Dutch", goes into its notes, never its instructions. The reply shows "Updated its notes +1 line" with View, which opens the notes in Agent settings, and Undo, which puts the notes back unless they changed since. With memory off the agent keeps nothing and says so, and "Add to instructions" opens its Instructions page with the line ready for you to add.
- Agent settings open beside the agent's DM, from the Agent settings door or the face in the header. A short root holds the face, name, title, what it helps with and its model, with a page each for instructions, memory, skills, connectors, scheduled tasks and access, where subagents sit. Memory holds the conversation summary and the agent's notes, and Scheduled tasks is where you give the agent a schedule; both were separate doors beside the DM and now live here. The model field is the composer's picker, with Fast mode in it. Changes save as you make them, quietly unless a save fails. Session agents open the same settings in Settings → Agents, listed first. The long agent form is gone.
- Tool groups are gone. Every agent gets every built-in tool; connections stay per agent, and writes to outside services still ask you first.
- A new agent opens its DM with three or four question cards, then writes its own instructions from your answers, once. After that only you change them. Its first message greets you by name, says what it left out and makes a suggestion or asks a question about its job. Skipping every question still leaves instructions built from the form.
- New agent opens a short form over the workspace: a face, a name, what it should help with, and a model with effort. Create opens the agent's DM. A new agent starts with memory on. The onboarding's first agent uses the same form. The Role field is gone from creation.
- A persona's face now shows what her turn is doing. She tilts her head while she thinks, follows lines while she reads, melts into three dots while her answer streams in, dozes while she waits on a colleague, turns to you when she needs an answer, nods when she is done and shakes when a turn fails. Any other work keeps the eye loop she already had. Reduced motion and photo avatars show none of it. The phone shows it on the face behind the latest reply and in a DM's working line.
- Persona faces have a richer finish. The light on a face is now tinted from its own colour, and small faces are drawn simpler with larger eyes.
- After you push a branch, the Session panel shows the pull or merge request page your remote offers, one click away. It needs no sign in in Manor.
- A push that fails because this Mac is not signed in to the remote now says so and names the way out, instead of repeating Git's last line.
- The README says that agents use your own git and `gh` sign in.
- An agent's own `git push` over ssh now works with a key the macOS ssh agent holds, as it does in your Terminal.
- A new Session can work in a folder without making a project. On an empty install the chip reads Add folder and opens the folder picker. Folders stay in the list until you remove them, which keeps their Sessions. Projects' + menu turns a folder into a project with a thread. The picker no longer creates projects.

## 0.1.0, 2026-11-13

The first public version. Manor is source only for now, so you build the app yourself. See `README.md`.
