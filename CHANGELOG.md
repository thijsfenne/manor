# Changelog

What changed for someone who uses Manor, newest first. A version is a tag on `main`. The root, server, web, desktop, contracts and client runtime packages carry the same number. The phone companion and the blobatar copy keep their own.

## Unreleased

- Connecting the phone takes three steps: Tailscale on the Mac and the phone, open the app, scan the QR code in Settings → Phone. That page turns Tailscale HTTPS on and off, makes a one-time link that works for 10 minutes on one device, and lists paired devices with Revoke, which disconnects that device at once. The link also pairs a phone's browser. The password account and the server address in the phone build are gone. Tailscale installed as the Mac app alone now works too, and the page never offers a localhost link, which no phone can open. A link on the Mac's network address still works.
- On the phone, the switches (such as All, Media and Files in a chat's files, the Plugins sections, Scheduled tasks and the chat mode on a new Session) are pills with one marker that slides to your choice, and it stays still when Reduce Motion is on. A switch that has a row to itself fills the row with equal parts. The mode switch on a proposal card keeps its own width, because it shares its row with a label.
- When one agent messages another, the agent that received it now knows in its own chat with you what it was told and what it answered. It no longer says no message arrived. It reads only the threads it is part of, and the exchange still lives only in that agent thread.
- On the phone, every button on a card (proposal, result, question, permission, secret, mail and Slack draft, and the file viewer's Open and Download) is Liquid Glass. The main action, such as Accept, Confirm and Send, is tinted glass so it stays the clear choice. Where glass is not available the buttons keep a flat fill.
- A Session whose turn ended while a background command or helper it started still runs now reads Waiting instead of Working, without a spinner. The project overview, on the Mac and on the phone, shows such Sessions in a Waiting section with what they wait on, such as the command's own description. The section only shows while a Session waits. When the work ends the Session picks up again and reads Working, or Done if nothing follows.
- A subagent's own view no longer shows the Session's Settle button, the button that opens the Session panel or the panel itself, even when the panel was open. Open parent or the breadcrumb brings all three back as you left them, and the panel shortcuts do nothing meanwhile. The phone's subagent screen never had them.
- When background work an agent started finishes, its line in the chat now says so in plain words, such as Background task finished: retry the phone launch, using the short description the agent gave the command. A command without one shows its program and first argument instead of the whole script, and three or more read as one count, such as 3 background tasks finished. The line stays at most two lines, on the Mac and on the phone.
- A Session in a project without a screenshot route (no verify skill, nor the skill that makes one) no longer needs a screenshot to report UI work. A passing test, typecheck or build is enough, and when no screenshot showed the change the result card says Not checked on screen. The first time such a Session works on UI, it asks once whether to add Cursor's open source verify skills to the project. Yes adds them with that Session's work. No means Manor never asks again in that project.
- On the phone, Request changes on a Session's result card takes pictures: paste one into the field and it shows as a small thumbnail with its own remove, and goes to the Session with your text. While you write, the card stands above the keyboard in place of the message field, so only one field is on screen. Cancel, Send or tapping away puts the card back in the thread where it was.
- The phone no longer flashes Offline when its connection to the Mac drops for a moment, such as when Manor restarts or the app comes back from the background. It shows Connecting… and reads Offline only after ten seconds without a connection. Coming back after more than an hour no longer fails the first reconnect on an expired sign-in, and a connection that went silent or hangs is replaced within seconds. The Mac app shows the same.
- A line in an agent's standing notes may now hold 300 characters, its date included, instead of 160. Lines saved before stay as they are.
- A project's Notes no longer show Manor's hidden source tags as text. A task that stayed open and unchanged for three days or more shows how long, such as open 4d, beside it. The project thread now reads that age on the task itself instead of a second stale copy of it above the list.
- On the phone, a project thread no longer jumps up and down when a proposal or result card arrives or changes. At the bottom it moves once to keep the card in view.
- A tasks card whose tasks are all done shows closed, as just its header with the check, the heading and the count, on the Mac and on the phone. It folds shut when the last task is ticked, and the chevron opens it again. A card you opened or closed yourself keeps that while it stays on screen, and a card that ended with tasks not done stays open.

## 0.2.0, 2026-11-17

- Pictures an agent hands over show only as pictures, without a file row with Open and Save under them. Other files, such as a PDF or a spreadsheet, keep their row. Pressing an agent's picture opens the same viewer as a DM's Files, over just that message's pictures, with Download to save one. On the Mac and on the phone.
- The cards inside a turn, questions, permission requests, secret requests, connect cards and mail and Slack drafts, set their text at 14px in every column, so they sit quieter beside the reply. In a DM every card in the chat now runs as wide as a bubble may grow, so they line up with the replies.
- The name on a pinned agent stays centred under the face when its unread dot appears or goes. The dot now hangs beside the name, on the Mac and on the phone.
- An agent adds an event to your iCloud calendar without an approval card when Manor runs with full access. With Auto it goes through the same review as other connector writes, and with Confirm it asks as before, showing the event. A scheduled routine still cannot add an event.
- A chat's time lines stand only directly above a message. Lines between agents, such as 2 messages with an agent, and hidden or system rows no longer get a time of their own, so several times no longer stack on top of each other. The phone shows the same time lines in a DM as the Mac.
- Show source on a quote, and other jumps to a message, scroll only the conversation. With the chat already at its bottom, the whole window used to slide up and leave a dark band under the message field. The source message now also lights up briefly, as a search hit does.
- The phone's Sessions screen drops its own search field; the Search tab finds every Session. The button at the bottom right now reads New session, in dark glass on the light theme and light glass on the dark theme.
- The phone app opens on the Agents tab instead of Sessions. Sessions is one tap away in the tab bar.
- Confirm on a Session that moved a file now also removes it from its old place. The commit used to add the file at its new path and leave the old copy behind, so a phone app built from such a commit could crash on launch.
- The decisions on a Session's result card read as a plain question, such as whether Manor should clean something up on its own, instead of a short internal label, on the Mac and on the phone. Their options say what happens, and an agent no longer asks you about details it can settle itself.
- A turn that changed more than 40 files shows every small file's diff. The files after the fortieth used to say partial and too large to show, even at one line. When a very wide turn does run out of room, the largest files say their diff was not loaded, and only a hunk that really is too large still says so.
- Persona faces no longer draw a shadow under them, or a glow in the dark theme. The light and shade on the face itself stay as they were, on the Mac and on the phone.
- One Stop halts the whole message. Every agent it addressed that has not started yet is cancelled too, the ones you mentioned by name included. Each used to need its own Stop.
- The Single and Multiple switch on a new Session explains itself in a tooltip when you point at it. The line under the message field is gone.
- The sidebar names its sections Project threads and Standalone sessions, on the Mac and on the phone. Adding a project thread opens New project thread, with one line on what it is.
- An agent that had been quiet for weeks answers again. Claude Code deletes a saved conversation after 30 days, and from then on every message to that agent failed at once, without a word in a DM. When Claude Code or Grok no longer has the conversation, Manor now starts a fresh session from its own record of the chat and answers the same message.
- The menus share one row: the same height and corners, an icon in front of each action, and a tick on the choice in use. That covers the Browser's menus and More panels, where a choice read Selected or Current, and the plus, model and branch menus under the message field, where the chosen row was filled.
- The New session and Recent sessions menus beside the Browser take the arrow keys, Home and End, as the other menus do. They and the Browser menu now put focus on their first row the first time they open too, not only from the second time on.
- While an agent works through its steps, only the turn that changed redraws. Every step used to redraw every message on screen, on the phone and on the Mac, a few times a second in a busy turn.
- Coming back to a chat after the connection dropped loads only what was added or changed meanwhile, on the phone and on the Mac. It used to download the whole conversation again, megabytes for a long one. After a restart of Manor on your Mac the first open still loads everything.
- On the Mac, opening a subagent's view again, or a chat in a second pane, asks only for what was added since. Each open used to download the whole thread.
- On the phone, the label next to the plus names who you mention while an agent is still answering, as on the Mac. It read as unknown for every draft during a running turn.
- On the phone, an answer typed on a question card stops at the 2,000 characters the Mac allows. A longer one made Send do nothing, without a word. A question you skipped on the Mac now reads Skipped on the phone, as it does there, and no longer the sentence meant for the agent.
- On the phone, what you type under Request changes stays when its card scrolls out of view or you leave the chat. The field used to come back open and empty, and a picked Your call choice was lost with it.
- On the phone, typing redraws only the composer. Every character used to redraw the whole chat screen, its header and its list with it.
- On the phone, a reply that streams in redraws only its own text. The header, the composer, the menu and the list under the chat used to redraw with every piece of text, in a DM too, where the reply shows only once it is whole. Scrolling and typing stay smooth while an agent writes.
- On the phone, the cards in a chat notice when the connection to your Mac drops or returns. A mail card that was already on screen kept its Send and Discard buttons live while offline.
- On the phone, a chat you went back from shows its unread dot when a reply lands in it, and a run that ends there waits for you instead of settling itself. Coming back to the app on the inbox no longer reloads the last chat you had open.
- The phone app loads a third of the code it did. It bundled every icon in the set, about 6,000, to draw 174.
- A DM's composer is one rounded row at the bottom, in an empty DM too: a plus, the message and send. It grows into the usual card once the message is longer than a line or carries an attachment. The model picker left it, because a DM runs on the model its agent's settings name. Auto no longer picks a model for a DM. A DM that still carries an earlier pick shows it in Agent settings, and choosing a model there replaces it.
- A rule you give an agent in chat, such as "write follow-ups in Dutch", goes into its notes, never its instructions. The reply shows "Updated its notes +1 line" with View, which opens the notes in Agent settings, and Undo, which puts the notes back unless they changed since. With memory off the agent keeps nothing and says so, and "Add to instructions" opens its Instructions page with the line ready for you to add.
- Agent settings open beside the agent's DM, from the Agent settings door or the face in the header. A short root holds the face, name, title, what it helps with and its model, with a page each for instructions, memory, skills, connectors, scheduled tasks and access, where subagents sit. Memory holds the conversation summary and the agent's notes, and Scheduled tasks is where you give the agent a schedule; both were separate doors beside the DM and now live here. The model field is the composer's picker, with Fast mode in it. Changes save as you make them, quietly unless a save fails. Session agents open the same settings in Settings → Agents, listed first. The long agent form is gone.
- Tool groups are gone. Every agent gets every built-in tool; connections stay per agent, and writes to outside services still ask you first.
- A new agent opens its DM with three or four question cards, then writes its own instructions from your answers, once. After that only you change them. Its first message greets you by name, says what it left out and makes a suggestion or asks a question about its job. Skipping every question still leaves instructions built from the form.
- New agent opens a short form over the workspace: a face, a name, what it should help with, and a model with effort. Create opens the agent's DM. A new agent starts with memory on. The onboarding's first agent uses the same form. The Role field is gone from creation.
- A persona's face now shows what her turn is doing. She tilts her head while she thinks, follows lines while she reads, melts into three dots while her answer streams in, dozes while she waits on a colleague, turns to you when she needs an answer, nods when she is done and shakes when a turn fails. Any other work keeps the eye loop she already had. Reduced motion and photo avatars show none of it. The phone shows it on the face behind the latest reply and in a DM's working line.
- Persona faces have a richer finish. The light on a face is now tinted from its own colour, and small faces are drawn simpler with larger eyes.
- After you push a branch, the Session panel shows the pull or merge request page your remote offers, one click away. It needs no sign in in Manor.
- A push that fails because this Mac is not signed in to the remote now says so and names the way out, instead of repeating Git's last line.
- The README says that agents use your own git and `gh` sign in.
- An agent's own `git push` over ssh now works with a key the macOS ssh agent holds, as it does in your Terminal.
- A new Session can work in a folder without making a project. On an empty install the chip reads Add folder and opens the folder picker. Folders stay in the list until you remove them, which keeps their Sessions. Projects' + menu turns a folder into a project with a thread. The picker no longer creates projects.
